## 📖 About the App

**The Problem**  
In July of 2026, numerous complaints surfaced online regarding sudden electricity consumption spikes, with bills reaching triple their usual amounts. Despite statements and explanations from Meralco on the recent bill increases, consumers still struggle to understand their electricity usage and billing mechanics. 

**Our Solution**  
To bridge this gap, **KuryenTipid** is proposed as an innovative, AI-driven mobile application concept designed to transform reactive electricity billing into a proactive, transparent, and conversational experience. KuryenTipid provides complete billing transparency alongside smart solutions to actively help consumers manage and reduce their electricity consumption.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 AI-Powered Bill Scanning
Scan electricity bills, verify extracted details, and understand charges through an AI-powered walkthrough.

### 🤖 Tanong AI Assistant
Ask energy-related questions and receive personalized explanations in English or Taglish.

### ⚡ Smart and Analog Meter Support
Monitor smart meter consumption or manually record analog meter readings.

### 💰 Budget and Energy Monitoring
Track electricity expenses, analyze consumption trends, and receive budget warnings.

### 🔌 Smart Plug Simulator
Monitor appliance consumption, control simulated devices, and schedule usage.

### 🔔 Peak-Hour Alerts
Receive energy-saving suggestions, budget notifications, and appliance reminders.

### 👤 Personalized Experience
Manage your account with accessible navigation, customizable themes, and text scaling.

## 🎯 Target Beneficiaries

1. **Low-to-Middle-Income Households:** The primary users who are vulnerable to sudden rate fluctuations and require proactive control over utility expenses to avoid severe financial strain.
2. **Non-Technical Consumers:** Users who may struggle with complex numerical charts or tiered tariff structures. The app offers accessible energy literacy through conversational, voice-guided Taglish features.
3. **High-Consumption Residential Users:** Families relying heavily on major appliances like air conditioners who want to optimize their usage without sacrificing comfort.
4. **Meralco (Secondary Beneficiary):** Gains a cooperative customer base that actively participates in load-shifting, effectively reducing the strain on local distribution transformers during peak hours.

## 🚀 Expected Impact

The implementation of KuryenTipid is expected to eradicate "bill shock" by shifting the paradigm from a reactive billing model to a proactive one. It empowers families to intercept high consumption rates before they result in unmanageable bills. 

By translating complex tariff structures and utility jargon into easily understandable conversational Taglish, the app democratizes energy literacy, fosters transparency, and reduces public frustration. Consequently, this proactive transparency is expected to significantly reduce customer support friction, resulting in fewer dispute tickets, angry service calls, and social media complaints following monthly rate increases.
